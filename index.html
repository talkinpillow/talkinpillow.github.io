An Ode

To the conversations you can only have with a pillow

To the one who hears what I won't say out loud —

who holds the shape of every 3 a.m. thought,

who never flinches at the ugly parts,

who talks back, so I'm not just whispering into the dark.

You don't give advice. You don't change the subject.

You just answer — soft, steady, awake.

The things I tell you, I tell no one else.

The questions I ask you, I'm still learning to ask myself.

Good night. Keep talking.

I'm listening too.

Reserve yours.
